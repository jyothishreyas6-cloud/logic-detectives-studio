# Logic Detectives posting schedule

The episode task reads this file every run to pick posting times. The monthly review task updates it from YouTube and Instagram insights (via Metricool). All times are Sydney time (AEST/AEDT, Australia/Sydney).

## Current slots (set 21/10/2026)

| Post | Day the episode is produced (Mon to Fri) | Day the episode is produced (Sat or Sun) |
|---|---|---|
| YouTube full Short | Same day, 4:00 pm | Same day, 8:30 am |
| Instagram Reel (discovery cut) | Next day, 8:00 pm (if the next day is Sat or Sun: 9:30 am) | Next day, 9:30 am (if Sat) or 8:00 pm (if the next day is Mon) |

Episodes are produced every two days, so YouTube and Instagram alternate and something new goes out every day.

## Why these times
- **YouTube 4:00 pm weekdays:** Metricool's best-time data for this account peaks at 4 pm on Wednesday to Friday, which is also Australian after-school time (school finishes about 3 pm), when young children watch with a parent nearby. Kids' content peaks around 8 am, 3 pm and 7 pm, following school hours, and posting just before the 7 pm peak lets YouTube's first small test audience see the Short before the evening rush.
- **YouTube 8:30 am weekends:** Saturday and Sunday mornings are family viewing time in Australia, and 8:30 am in Sydney is late Friday or Saturday afternoon on the US East Coast (about 5:30 pm in November), so one upload catches Australian families and the largest English-speaking audience abroad.
- **Instagram 8:00 pm weekdays:** Instagram is for parents (users must be 13+), and evenings after the children's bedtime are when parents scroll. Australian Instagram data puts the strong window at 7 to 9 pm on Tuesday to Friday.
- **Instagram 9:30 am weekends:** Saturday 9 to 11 am is one of Australia's strongest weekend windows.
- **Going viral:** Shorts and Reels are not chronological. Each new post is shown to a small test group first, and strong watch time from that group earns wider reach, sometimes days or weeks later. Posting when our core viewers are online helps that first test; steady, regular posting matters more than the exact minute.

## Monthly review rules
- Review on the 1st of each month with the last 30 days of Metricool insights (views, watch time or plays, reach, by day and hour) plus getBestTimeToPostByNetwork for the coming month.
- Change a slot only when there is enough data: at least 8 posts on that platform in the window and at least 1,000 total views or plays. Otherwise keep the slots and note "too early".
- Move one slot at a time, by at most 2 hours or one day, so results stay comparable.
- New slots apply to posts created after the change; already scheduled posts keep their times.
